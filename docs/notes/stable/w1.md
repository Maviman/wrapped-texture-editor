## Item 1: `passed` / ends meet on open surfaces, the board's rotation

**Cause of the false `passed`** (probe `check_b_probe` with the logged placement bits + a temporary debug print):
the pass test fired on a sign change of the - head's offset along the + head's direction, whatever the heads did.
- noisy400 (open rough field): heads 21.5 apart, both traces running the **same way** (`d+ . d- = +0.95`): the + trace
  had turned 90 degrees on the rough surface.
- ABeautifulGame Jump on: heads 8.5 cm apart at different heights, `d+ . d- = +0.45`, offset 0.0001 -> -0.0004.
- Synthetic rough fields (200 x 200, amp 0.6; 5 of 12 seeds fired with round 9's rule): `d+ . d- = -0.51 / -0.91`
  but the offset **jumped** by 10.9 / 10.5 in one check while the traces went 1.1 / 0.7 (the + trace's 3D direction
  swung on a steep facet), heads 52 / 43 apart.
- Real passes: torus knot `-0.998`, WA32 `-0.93 / -0.93 / -0.66`; offset change = 0.9-1.0 x the arc length gone.

**Fix.** A pass needs `d+ . d- < PASS_FACING` (0: the heads come from opposite sides) and a continuous crossing (the
offset falls by at most `PASS_STEP` (2) x the arc length both traces went since the last check). The shared-triangle
rule (`met_itself`) had the same weakness (noisy400 after the pass fix: `met_itself` with the traces crossing each
other's path at cos +0.39, ends 88 %): each turn-0 triangle now keeps the first crossing's direction and sign; the
other trace's path closes only when entered from the other side (`cos < 0`), the own path only the same way round
(`cos > 0`), the anchor's triangle always (`closes_on`). A stricter "head-on within 60 degrees" rule broke WA21 (the
lathe piece's traces meet at the back at cos -0.35) and the check_a9 cone (pass at -0.66 is fine, but its meeting
needs cos < 0 only), so 0 was chosen. Results: noisy400 `passed false`, `met false`, ends 100 % (round 9: passed, 36 %);
torus knot, WA32 and check_a9 unchanged (fingerprint of torus_knot identical); 24 synthetic rough fields (200 and 400
side, 12 seeds) never close.

**The board's rotation has a different cause.** With the pass fixed (w1a exe) the board picture was still turned:
the chess set's exact region (T5) failed its check in one component (a pawn) and round 9's fallback dropped the exact
pins of **every** component, so the board (a strip component laid exactly from the spine's short run on it) was
solved freely from its Fermi start and turned (jump turns of 21-71 degrees logged for the parts jumped from the
board). Fix: the exact pins' check and fallback are per component (a jumped part re-placed from a changed source is
solved and checked again; one component gives round 9's result bit for bit). Measured on the board (the layout's
x gradient on the board's top triangles inside the picture against the projector's right projected on the board):
round 9 **37.9 degrees** mean, w1 **-2.1 degrees**; the board's share of the picture 0.616 -> 0.800. The shot
(`target\w1\z_abg_wide.png`) shows the rows parallel to the frame's top edge as in rounds 6-8.

ABeautifulGame Jump on now: `passed false, met_itself true`, ends 73.8 % (each trace loops round a pawn after its
spine jumps and meets its own path, cos 0.83 / 0.9999: a real closure round a part), covered 1.0, ink 0.8463 ->
0.9509, dup 2.674 -> **3.075 %** (limit 3 %), scatter 2.631 -> 2.505 %, flipped 69,120 -> 58,604, rejected 45.6 ->
43.6 %. See "Open" below for the dup.

**Tests.** `wrap_tests::stable::w1_an_open_rough_field_never_closes` (seeds 1 and 9 of the 200 field, which fire
with round 9's rule: the test fails with `WTE_TEST_R9_PASS=true`), `w1_a_part_on_a_plane_keeps_the_rows_on_the_plane`
(synthetic: lathe pieces standing on a floor, Jump on, anchor on a piece's base; rows within 5 degrees, floor share

the behaviour), `w1_the_chess_board_keeps_the_rows_under_a_pawn` (the real case from `ABeautifulGame.glb`, step 10's
placement bits; rows within 5 degrees, board share >= 0.7, `passed` false; fails with
`WTE_TEST_R9_FALLBACK=true WTE_TEST_R9_PASS=true`; **skipped** when the sample is not found: `WTE_GLB_SAMPLES`, or

## Item 3: reuse = fresh

- Layout: the exact pins' check used the unbucketed picture rectangle; it now uses the bucketed reach rectangle
  (`footprint`). Shown: with the old rectangle, WA35's bump plate scaled by 0.95 bakes differently reused vs fresh
  (the new test fails there); with the new one all sizes are equal. No other layout decision used the picture's own
  size (spine, front, jumps, early-out, owner grid use the bucketed reach; `reached_edge` was a stat).
- Stats: one function (`WrapParam::size_stats`) computes every size-dependent field from stored inputs, for the fresh
  build and for `stats_for` / `stats_for_src` (callers with the source at hand pass its size): covered (stored f32
  corners), reached_edge (Pareto front of final boundary vertices), ends_share (stored end sum), flipped, stretch (per
  stored triangle ratio and flip flag), seams / seams_by_rule / cut_tris (laid seam half-edges with f64 ends), rejected
  and lost_cut (per owner cell mark, owner part and pair counts). Memoised for the last size. Fresh stats move only at
  float rounding (nonmanifold covered 0.63026214 -> 0.6302622); every other sweep stat equal.
- The size statistics cost 13 ms per parameterization on a 1,024 x 525 owner grid at first (WaterBottle param 15 ->
  28 ms); `rejected_pairs` / `lost_cells` now work on the picture's cell rectangle and on runs of uncovered cells:
  2.7 ms, same counts (fingerprints with stats of 16 runs identical). Sweep params are now within +2-5 ms of round 9
  on the small models.
- Test: `w1_reuse_equals_fresh_within_the_bucket`: WA35 capped cylinder (7 of 10 scales reused), WA35 bump plate (7),
  WA32 torus (6), WA24 cube on the floor with Jump on (2); scales 0.84-1.18; each reused size baked and its stats
  compared with a fresh document of that size (bytes of every set, all stats but `micros`). The WA32 torus' stats
  differ between sizes (cut 41 / 46 / 50, seams 44 / 47), so round 9's stale stats fail this test too.
- Visible effect: many_islands Jump on reports ends 36.9 % and lost_cut 0.51 % for the stamp's own size; round 9
  logged 33.2 % / 1.48 % (the values of the size the cached parameterization was built for).

## Item 4: `lost_cut` and gaps between parts

An uncovered picture cell counts only when the nearest covered cells on both sides of its row (or column) are owned
by the **same part**. For one part this equals round 9's span rule cell for cell (Lantern 11.23 % in both, all
single-part sweep values unchanged). tiles 40 x 40 (test) 17.06 % -> 0; tiles300 16.85 % -> 0; DamagedHelmet Jump
on 2.61 % -> 2.01 %. Test: `w1_lost_cut_does_not_count_the_gaps_between_parts` (fails on round 9's rule: 0.1706).

## Item 5: tiled UV triangles

A footprint whose UV box spans more than one texture (<= `UV_SHARE_TILED_MAX` = 4 textures) is rasterized wrapped,
each texel once; it shares when it covers a texel twice by itself or more than half its texels are counted twice.
(With 64 the shared texels of ngons_tiling.obj, UVs tiled up to 4 x 4, were not built within the whole wrap_surface
run: the panel showed no line where round 9 showed 100 %; with 4: 250 ms on the warm-up thread, 100 % as round 9.)
Beyond 4: a triangle holding a whole texture (inscribed circle >= sqrt 2) counts everywhere (as round 9), a huge
sliver marks only itself (not counted for others: a possible miss, preferred to a false 100 %). Test
`uv_share::tests::w1_a_tiled_trim_strip_does_not_mark_a_unique_island` (plate + strip u 0..12: plate not shared,
strip shared, share 12/13; a 3 x 3 texture quad still marks the plate; a 10,000-wide sliver only itself). WA39 and
the mesh shares unchanged (ABeautifulGame 23.1 %, DamagedHelmet 86.7 %, ngons_tiling 100 %). The skeptic's
`trim_strip.obj` in the app (`wrap_surface`): mesh share 23.1 % (the strip), the picture on the plate 0.0 %, no line
(round 9: 100 %, line shown).
