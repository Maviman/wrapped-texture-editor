## Item 1: exact parts on a cylinder next to a cap

**Cause** (check:A of round 9, section 5, confirmed with `WTE_TEST_EXACT_DEBUG`): the front's seed is not isometric on
the side rows next to the dome; round 9's exact region pins only seed-isometric vertices (49 wedges), so the rows next
to the rim are solved and ARAP spreads the dome's strain into them.

**Fix** (`wrap_relax.rs`, `Layout::develop_exact`): the exact region is grown by **developing** the surface from it
instead of trusting the seed. From the laid triangles whose corners are all exact or strip pins and isometric there,
each laid triangle across an uncut manifold edge is unfolded rigidly onto that edge (lowest `g` first), but only when
one of its corners is **flat**: a closed laid fan with `|deficit| <= FLAT_TOL` (1e-6 rad) inside a flat area (a triangle
round it has three flat corners). The cylinder's rim vertices are curved (the dome), so no dome triangle is developed,
but every side triangle touching the rim is (it has a flat side corner). A triangle whose third corner already has a
position joins only when isometric there; afterwards every laid triangle whose corners are all pinned must be isometric
(else its developed corners are freed). The check / fallback gets a middle step: a component whose developed pins fail
keeps round 9's region only, then the strip pins only.

Numbers (WA35): capped cylinder side **99.920 % within 2/255, max 4** (round 9 92.368 %, max 51), exact wedges 49 ->
106, seams 0, scatter 0, rejected 0. Bump plate: the developed pins fail the strain check (the 45 degree bump inside a
fully pinned plain has more surface than its rim encloses) and the middle step keeps round 9's region: flat texels
100 %, max 1 (as before). 20 degree cone: falls back to the strip pins, bit-equal to the strip-only result (as before).
`FLAT_TOL`: rounding leaves at most 1.4e-8 rad on the samples' flat / cylindrical / conical vertices; the first value
(2e-4) let WA4's curved plates (|deficit| 1.5e-5 .. 9.6e-3) develop along lines where the curvature crosses zero (WA4
45 degrees went red: cells 1.308); with 1e-6 and the flat-area rule WA4 is bit-identical to round 9 on this point
(`w2_the_developed_region_stays_off_curved_surface`).

**WaterBottle (round-6 project, tint change): model-caused, not reachable by an exact region.** Probe
`target\w2\compat_probe` (a copy of the gate's, built against this tree), deficits dumped in `wb_deficits.txt`:
- The label ("wf_stamp.png") lies on the shoulder: every closed vertex of its patch has `|deficit| >= 4.9e-3 rad`
  (231 closed vertices, none flat; normals 0.08-0.68 off horizontal). The "body" texels of the gate's measure
  (`|n . axis| < 0.05`) are the shoulder's lowest band, still curved (deficit 1.3e-2 at |n.y| 0.08).
- The text "WTE" lies on the body over a profile ring at y = -0.032 with deficit 0.111 rad per vertex (a bend of the
  bottle's profile); 2 of 68 closed vertices are flat.
- So no isometric layout exists there; round 6 kept its seed (one unfolding among many), round 9+ relaxes. Results
  with item 1 only: logo 71.79 % (body 68.14 %), text 50.77 % (body 53.46 %) - unchanged, as expected (no flat area).
  After all w2 changes: see `compat_w2.txt` (below, "Verification").

## Item 2: tests that demanded the impossible

**WA17 / WA24.** The picture is now centred at z = 0.95 (rounds 6-9: 1.2). From 1.2 its last 0.2 of 2.0 lay beyond the
floor's edge (z = 2): at most 0.90 could lie once (check:A section 2). From 0.95 it runs from z = 1.95 on the floor
(1.45) up the cube's side (0.55 of its 1.0 height): all of it has surface. Limits unchanged. WA17: ink 1.014, cube
23,001 texels, jumps 1, smear 0.004 %. WA24: ink 1.0142, dup 0, cube 23,001 texels; its grid half (never reached
before): ink 0.9980, dup 0, 1 part entered (limit 5) - green.

**Step-10 coverage assert.** New `WrapStats::room` = min(1, the largest 3D extent along the picture's across axis of
the parts its stored triangles belong to / the picture's across size), stored per parameterization
(`WrapParam::part_across`) and recomputed per size. The assert asks `covered >= 0.85 x ends_share x room` unless
`reached_edge`. Sweep: ABeautifulGame Jump off covered 0.0731 vs 0.85 x 0.3314 x 0.2425 = 0.0683 (a pawn a quarter of
the picture's height); many_islands off 0.0177 vs 0.0143 (room 0.091), on 0.0338 vs 0.0323 (room 0.103). For every
part as tall as the picture `room` is 1 and the assert is round 9's. The 3D extent is a lower bound of the surface's
room across (a curved part has more surface than its extent), so the exemption errs on the side of the assert.

**Lantern: topology-caused (a coarse closed box), evidence:**
- The laid set is the whole base block (100 triangles, 52 vertices, no open vertex: `wraps_closed`): a picture 12.1 x
  6.1 on a block 4.7 high and about 3.5 wide.
- A closed surface cannot lie flat: its total curvature (720 degrees) must go somewhere. Deficits
  (`lantern_seams.txt`): plinth bottom corners +121.2 deg (cones, cut), body top corners +93.4 (cut), the block's foot
  where it meets the plinth -92.1 / -78.7 deg next to the plinth's +92.1 corners 0.07 away (a step: T3's net rule
  sees them cancel and does not cut them). S5 (19 edges) only joins the cones into one cut tree, which on a closed
  surface it must; on a 2-triangle face the only edges across are its diagonals, so some cuts run through faces.
- The two thin bare slivers in the front faces are not cuts: they are the two triangles (`lantern_relax.txt`: tri 19
  and 40, laid at 8 % and 14 % of their 3D area, partly beyond the picture's edge) that absorb the foot's -92 degree
  excess on a 2-triangle face; one is flipped. Cutting every |deficit| > 45 vertex instead (round 8's rule,
  `WTE_TEST_CONE_RADIUS=0`) is worse: lost_cut 14.7 %, rejected 35 %, 13 flipped.
- So `lost_cut` (11.2 %; 12.1 % with item 3's solver) is the closed box's curvature, not a defect of the cut rules.
  New `WrapStats::wraps_closed` (a part without boundary edges all of whose triangles are laid on the first turn); the
  step-10 assert keeps `lost_cut <= 10 %` unless `wraps_closed`; the panel's cut line says "P % of the picture is lost
  where it is cut open round the closed part's corners" then.

## Item 4: bare areas from the place owner

Probe `wrap_tests::w2_owner_probe` (ignored; placement bits from the sweep logs): on a 240 x 120 grid of picture points,
the owner's place and the dropped places, each tested for being hidden along the projector's `out` (a BVH ray).

- **nonmanifold (a book: seven pages on one edge, one part): a clear bug.** The owner gave the picture's far half to
  pages behind the one the stamp faces: owner hidden at 2,253 of 5,186 points, 1,551 of them with a visible place
  dropped (the front page bare in the shot). Round 9 never asked: `buried` is only evaluated in "mixed" cells
  (candidates of two parts), and the pages are one part.
- **DamagedHelmet Jump on: mostly model-caused.** 28,066 points, 4,610 with another place: 3,586 of them two *visible*
  places laid at one picture point (the helmet is an assembly of overlapping shell plates; Jump lays each plate from
  its landing and their layouts overlap where the plates do), 1,525 visible owner over a hidden place (right), 376
  hidden owner over a visible place (the bug above, 1.3 % of the points). Plus 86.7 % mirrored / shared UVs (paint
  shows on the mirrored half too; panel line shown). check:A's experiment (a longer bury ray) changed nothing.

**Fix** (`wrap_param.rs` `make_layer`, `OwnerCtx::hidden_at`; `wrap_relax.rs` `OwnerTri::sheet`): every stored triangle
carries its **sheet** (triangles joined over edges with exactly two triangles); a cell is mixed when its candidates lie
on two sheets at two places (round 9: two parts). In a mixed cell a candidate is buried also when a ray from its point
along the projector's `out` hits an entered part's triangle (its own part included) within 2 diag. `Burial::may` is
then true for every triangle (the old prism test missed hidden pages). Results: nonmanifold owner hidden 2,253 -> 43,
hidden-over-visible 1,551 -> 0 (`w2_a_book_shows_the_picture_on_the_page_it_faces`, fails with
`WTE_TEST_OWNER_VISIBLE=false`); DamagedHelmet 376 -> 278 (the rest: one sheet at two places, a fold); ABeautifulGame
Jump on owner phase 188 -> 200 ms. Shot (`nm_cmp.png`): the front page now shows cells over two thirds of its width
(w1: stripes only).

**Side effect, red:** on the book the picture's far half is now split between two visible pages (the front page and
the one left of it), so more picture bins are found at two places: nonmanifold dup 1.52 % -> **3.41 %** (step-10 limit
3 %), scatter 0.54 -> 2.00 %. The book has no single right place for that half (seven pages share the spine edge and
the picture overlaps several of them). Left red and reported; the knob `WTE_TEST_OWNER_SHEETS=false` (test builds)
gives round 9's mixed rule for comparison.

Panel: the lines of w1 already explain both (DamagedHelmet: "18 % of the laid picture lies on surface hidden under
another part or folded over itself"; the book: 70 %). New lines: "The part is narrower than the picture: only P % ..."
when `room < 0.95` (many_islands), and the closed-part cut line (Lantern).
